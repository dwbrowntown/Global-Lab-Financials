How the numbers are built

The rule. An order's full cost sits in the fiscal quarter its last part ships. Costs are never split across quarters.

Sched ship in the orders list is the latest scheduled ship date of the lines still to ship, the planning date until an actual ship date posts. Greyed when every line has shipped.

Shipped lines use their actual ship date. Unshipped lines use their scheduled ship date. If that date has passed or is blank, the line counts as shipping today, so a late order stays in the current quarter and rolls forward until it actually ships. The order lands on the latest of its line dates.

Credits. Return lines land in the quarter they're received, or the quarter they were posted until then.

Cost. Item standard cost × ordered quantity. Line Amount USD is $0 on internal orders, so it isn't used.

Envelope. GL account prefix: 2xxxx is CapEx, 8xxxx is OpEx. Finance's underlying test is a new asset above $5,000, so some orders get reclassed later.

Scope. Lines with a 5-digit account, not cancelled, expense dept starting 7030, 7110, 7120, 7130 or 7140. No sales-channel filter, since it's blank after the Fusion cutover.

Old-system orders. Open lines still sitting on old Oracle EBS orders (5017xxx) are left out. At the Fusion cutover those lines were rebooked or cancelled on a Fusion order, so counting them again would double the cost. Lines that actually shipped on an EBS order stay in, since that history only exists there.

Status. Green below 70% of the allowance, Yellow 70 to 90, Red 90 to 100, Over above 100.

Budget quarter vs order placed. Budget quarter is what hits the allowance. Order placed shows what was raised that quarter, wherever it lands.

Not included. GL actuals (row access policy on EDNA_GL_TRANSACTION), third-party amortisation, and asset grouping by project code.

Order search. Typing an SO or part number in the orders list searches every order loaded on this page, from any quarter, open or closed, and pauses the quarter, envelope and department filters. Clearing the box, or picking a quarter, brings the quarter view back. For an order that is not loaded here, such as an older quarter, press Enter or use Search Snowflake to pull it live. Those rows are tagged Snowflake and never change a total on this page.
