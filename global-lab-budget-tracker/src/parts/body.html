How the numbers are built

The budget rule. An order's full cost sits in the fiscal quarter its last part ships. Costs are never split across quarters in the Budget quarter view.

GL timing. Each line's cost sits in the quarter that line ships, which is how inventory relief posts to the GL. Use it to tie out to Finance's GL detail. Orders that ship across a quarter end show part of their cost in each quarter.

Shipped lines use their actual ship date. Unshipped lines use their scheduled ship date. If that date has passed or is blank, the line counts as shipping today, so a late order stays in the current quarter and rolls forward until it actually ships.

Credits. Return lines land in the quarter they're received, or the quarter they were posted until then.

Cost. Item standard cost times ordered quantity. Line Amount USD is $0 on internal orders, so it isn't used.

Envelope. From the approved account list in settings.js. An account not on the list falls back to the prefix rule (2xxxx CapEx, otherwise OpEx) and shows on the watch list until Finance places it.

Asset test. Finance capitalizes a new asset over $5,000, one asset per project per location. CapEx with no project and under $5,000 is at risk of reclass to expense. OpEx over $5,000 with no project may belong in CapEx.

Scope. Lines with a 5-digit account, not cancelled, expense dept starting 7030, 7110, 7120, 7130 or 7140. Open lines left on old Oracle EBS orders are excluded, since they were rebooked on Fusion orders at cutover.

Third-party spend. From EDNA procurement at requisition grain. Converted to USD at the rates in settings.js. Lands in the quarter it was approved, unless settings say it is still open (it then rolls with the current quarter until received) or on hold. Currency code 0 is inferred from the requisition number format.

Support and maintenance. Anne's rule: support at or above $30,000 a year goes to prepaid and is amortized monthly over the term; below that it is expensed when posted. Terms come from the contract list in settings.js.

Status. Green below 70% of the allowance, Yellow 70 to 90, Red 90 to 100, Over above 100. Allowances come from settings.js by fiscal year.

Order search. Typing an SO or part number searches every order loaded on this page and pauses the quarter filters. Press Enter or use Search Snowflake to pull an order that isn't loaded. Those rows are tagged Snowflake and never change a total.
